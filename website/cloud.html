FilterMate stays entirely free and open source. Only what runs on servers may become paid, later.

Our commitments

Five rules we keep

They hold whatever happens to the paid offer.

  1. No feature of the plugin is ever locked or time-limited.
  2. Everything paid runs on a server and brings what your computer alone cannot do.
  3. The account is optional: your own key and Ollama keep working without one.
  4. Nothing goes to a server unless you turned it on; what is sent is written down.
  5. Reminders of the paid offer are rare and placed where they help.
