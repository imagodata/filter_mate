FilterMate 5.1 is out for QGIS.

Ask in one sentence. Check every step. Undo in one click.

The new AGENT section (experimental) filters, selects, runs Processing, styles layers and exports from a request, in your project:
• Each filter says how many features it keeps; exports and removals are confirmed.
• Four levels of autonomy, a rule per tool, layers it must never touch.
• Every request is one turn you can undo.
• Claude, Mistral, OpenAI or a local Ollama model, your own key, no account. A few free requests a month to try it.
• Recipes replay a request without any model: nothing sent, nothing paid.

Also experimental: objects drawn by SAM on your CPU, which then filter your other layers.

Free and open source, QGIS 3.22 to 4.x. The article walks through it:
https://imagodata.github.io/filter_mate/#agent
