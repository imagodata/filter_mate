FilterMate 5 connects vector, raster and LiDAR selections inside QGIS.

The article walks through five focused tasks in Sarlat-la-Canéda:
• A 50 m street corridor: 113 buildings and 106 parcels.
• One survey boundary for a DTM and LiDAR cloud.
• Parcels with at least 50% coverage by slopes ≥ 20%.
• Parcels with at least 50% coverage by an NDVI > 0.3 selection.
• High-vegetation proximity on a scoped boulevard network.

Each example includes the normal QGIS window and a larger widget detail, so you can read the source criteria, targets, predicate, buffer and coverage settings.

Free and open source: https://github.com/imagodata/filter_mate
