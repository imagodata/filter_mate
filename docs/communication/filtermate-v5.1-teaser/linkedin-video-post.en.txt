One question, one click: the 360,577 buildings of Toulouse Métropole, filtered across 12 layers, inside QGIS.

That is FilterMate 5.1 in 55 seconds (video below, sound on):

→ Vector, raster and point cloud layers, each as a source or a target
→ Filters written in the SQL of each source: PostGIS, GeoPackage, SpatiaLite, files
→ Chained filters (AND, OR, AND NOT), on several targets at once
→ LiDAR: the buildings over 7 m, read from the point cloud
→ Undo, Redo, and a Compare line that shows the map before and after
→ Favorites, an export of only the answer, Processing and an API to automate

Two new sections, both experimental:

→ SAM: one click outlines an object on the orthophoto, on your CPU, and the object filters your other layers
→ AGENT: ask in one sentence, check every step, undo in one click. With your own API key, or a local Ollama model: in the video the model runs on my laptop, and nothing leaves it.

Everything in the video is the real plugin in QGIS 4.2, recorded on open data of the IGN.

FilterMate is free and open source, for QGIS 3.22 to 4.x. Links in the first comment.
