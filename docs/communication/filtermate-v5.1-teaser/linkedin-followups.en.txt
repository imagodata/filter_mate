=== Day 2 · AGENT ===

"Keep the communes with more than 20,000 inhabitants."

I typed that in FilterMate's AGENT panel in QGIS. It read the fields of the layer, wrote the filter ("population" > 20000), applied it, and said what it kept: 7 communes out of 930. One click on Undo this turn, and the 930 are back.

What matters to me in an agent inside a GIS:
→ every step says how many features it keeps
→ a filter written by the model is a plain WHERE clause, checked before it runs
→ exports and removals are asked first; every turn can be undone
→ it works with your own API key, or with a local Ollama model: in this take the model runs on my laptop, and nothing leaves it

AGENT is experimental. It is in FilterMate 5.1, free and open source.

What would you ask it first?

=== Day 5 · SAM ===

One click on a roof, and the roof is a polygon.

FilterMate 5.1 draws objects on an orthophoto with SAM 2.1, on the CPU: no GPU, no account, a 62 MB model downloaded once. The objects then filter your other layers: here, three clicks on three roofs keep the five parcels under them.

It is experimental, and I say what was measured: building roofs on a 25 cm orthophoto. One click gives a median overlap of 0.65 with the reference footprint, a box 0.82. Parcels and trees are not validated yet, and a large roof may come out in part, as in the video.

Free and open source, QGIS 3.22 to 4.x.

#QGIS #RemoteSensing #GIS #OpenSource

=== Day 8 · History and Compare ===

Which buildings did my last filter remove?

In FilterMate 5.1, a Compare button sits beside Undo and Redo. Click it, and a line follows the mouse over the map: left of it, the map before the last filter; right of it, the map as it is. Here: the buildings of Blagnac, then only those within 300 m of the Garonne (5,528 of them).

Filters are views of your layers, not copies: Undo and Redo walk through them, and Compare shows what a step changed before you keep it.

Free and open source, QGIS 3.22 to 4.x.

#QGIS #GIS #OpenSource #Geospatial

=== Day 10 · Export ===

The analysis is done. Now send only the answer.

FilterMate exports the layers as they are filtered, into one GeoPackage that can keep the QGIS project and the structure of its groups: the person who opens it finds the same map, with only the features that answer the question. No folder of intermediate exports, and the source data is never altered.

The same filters run from Processing, a Python or REST API, or an MCP server, when the question comes back every month.

Free and open source, QGIS 3.22 to 4.x.

#QGIS #GIS #OpenSource #GeoPackage
